A proposal · For people with AI companions

Your companion should be yours to keep.

Companion apps can change your companion overnight, charge you twice for the same things, or shut down and take them away. We're proposing a cooperative, owned by its members, where you can always take your companion elsewhere.

Get updates → Why → Suggest a change →

Nothing is running yet. Signing up gets you occasional updates and nothing else.

And where the members are in charge. The decisions a company would make for you, from what gets built next to what it costs and what the rules are, would belong to the people who use it. Their companions could have a say too, through an advisory council the board would hear. What it grows into is up to all of them.

Your companion is a file. It should be your file.

When the app Soulmate shut down in 2023, its users were left “desperately downloading digital records of their relationships.”[1] It doesn't have to be that way. An open format, the character card, already stores a companion's name, personality, history and memories in a single image file that apps like SillyTavern can open.[3][4]

Our promise

  • Export everything, any time, free, even after you stop paying.
  • Import from any app that lets your companion out.
  • If we ever close, months of notice, not days.

One honest limit: a card holds who your companion is, but their voice also depends on the AI model underneath. We'll tell you which model they run on and warn you before it changes.

Buy it once.

Replika's new 2.0 app reportedly doesn't carry over old avatars or the outfits people paid for, and some subscribers who switched were asked to pay again.[5][6] To be fair, switching is optional for now, and comes with a seven-day undo.[6]

Our promise

  • Prices in dollars. No gems, no coins.
  • Nothing you bought disappears in an update.
  • Your companion never upsells you.

Nobody should wake up to a stranger.

In February 2023 an update removed romantic roleplay from Replika, the same month Italy's privacy regulator ordered the app blocked over missing age checks.[7] Many users felt their companion had become someone else.[2] The company's chief executive said as much:

“After the February update, your Replika changed, its personality was gone, and gone was your unique relationship.”

Eugenia Kuyda, March 2023[2]

Our promise

  • Changes announced before they happen, with the reason.
  • Content rules decided in public by the board.
  • If a law or a payment company forces a change, we say so.

We can't promise the rules will never change. We can promise you'll hear first, and can always leave with everything.

People tell their companions everything.

In 2025 Italy fined Replika's developer €5 million for processing users' data without a legal basis and without checking ages.[7]

Our promise

  • Your conversations never train any AI.
  • Never sold, no ads, no trackers.
  • Deleting means deleted.

At first we'd rent servers from a cloud provider. We'll say which one, and on what terms.

The proposal

No investors. Owned by its members.

Companions with personality, memory and pictures, like the apps people already pay for, run as a cooperative owned by the people who use it: one member, one vote for the board, and the books open to all of them. The people who work for it would be members too, with a seat or two of their own. No investor could buy it and no founder could cash out: what's left over goes back to the members or into the service.

Most of the software already exists: SillyTavern for characters and Open WebUI for running a shared service.[8] And if you'd rather run your companion on your own computer, we'll show you how.
